# Pricing & Packaging Proposal

This proposal is for the next paid alpha phase, not a broad public launch.

## Positioning Rule

Sell **coherence and finishability**, not generic AI magic.

The product is strongest when it is framed as:

> The AI workspace for building concept albums that actually hold together.

That means pricing should map to creative progress and export value, not to vague "AI credits" alone.

## Recommended Packaging

### Free

Use Free as the evaluation lane, not the final destination.

- Price: `$0`
- Target user: curious creator evaluating the workflow
- Goal: get them to one real album draft fast

**What to include**
- Up to `5` projects
- Quick-start album scaffold
- Album bible and studio editing
- Version history
- Publish/discover browsing
- JSON export only

**What to hold back**
- Full export bundle
- Higher credit limits
- Team/admin workflows

### Pro

Pro should be the main public offer during paid alpha.

- Price: `$12/mo`
- Target user: solo artist, producer-songwriter, serious album builder
- Goal: convert users who reach first real momentum

**What to include**
- Unlimited projects
- Full export bundle: MIDI, ChordPro, MusicXML, JSON
- Higher credit limits
- Comments, tasks, notifications, versions
- Publish and remix workflows

**Why it works**
- The price is low enough for indie creators
- The value is concrete: real project throughput plus export handoff
- It aligns with the current product surface better than selling "team collaboration" aggressively

### Team

Do not lead with Team publicly yet.

- Internal placeholder price exists today: `$29/mo`
- Recommendation: keep it as a soft-launch or manual pilot plan only

**Reason**
- The repo has workspaces, comments, tasks, notifications, and billing groundwork
- It does **not** yet have a mature collaboration story for invites, seat management, role templates, or real-time co-editing
- Selling Team hard before those pieces exist will create expectation debt

**How to handle Team during alpha**
- Mention it only in sales conversations
- Offer it manually to small bands or writing teams willing to work closely with you
- Position it as a pilot, not a finished team product

## Recommended Public Pricing Table

| Plan | Price | Best for | Core promise |
|------|-------|----------|--------------|
| Free | `$0` | Trying the workflow | Build your first coherent album blueprint |
| Pro | `$12/mo` | Serious solo creators | Finish more complete album projects and export them cleanly |
| Team | Manual / pilot | Small creative teams | Shared album planning with hands-on support |

## Packaging Principles

### 1. Lead with one hero plan

During paid alpha, the hero offer should be `Pro`.

Too many plans create noise. The current strongest story is:

- Free to evaluate
- Pro to do real work
- Team only when the use case is strong enough to justify hands-on onboarding

### 2. Gate by outcome, not complexity

Good gates for this product:

- project limits
- export formats
- credit ceilings
- admin/team workflows

Bad gates for this product:

- hiding the core album bible
- making the editor feel broken
- turning the free plan into a toy

### 3. Keep pricing legible

Creators should be able to explain the value in one sentence:

> I pay because it helps me build a coherent album and export it into my real production workflow.

If the price forces a longer explanation, the packaging is too complicated.

## Founder Offer Recommendation

For the first `10-25` paid alpha users:

- Keep the monthly price at `$12/mo`
- Offer direct onboarding and feedback access
- Promise founder pricing protection for at least `6-12` months

This avoids discounting the product into irrelevance while still rewarding early risk-taking.

## Messaging by Plan

### Free
"Start your first concept album and see if the workflow clicks."

### Pro
"Build full album blueprints, keep the project coherent, and export clean handoff packs."

### Team
"Run shared concept development with manual support while the collaboration layer matures."

## What Not To Sell Yet

Do not center the following claims in public pricing copy yet:

- deep team administration
- polished multi-seat management
- real-time co-editing
- finished-audio generation
- enterprise-ready workflow automation

Those may become true later, but they are not the wedge now.

## Upgrade Trigger

The best upgrade prompt is not "You ran out of credits."

The best upgrade prompt is:

> Your album is taking shape. Upgrade to export the full bundle and keep building without project limits.

That aligns payment with visible creative progress.

## Recommendation Summary

- Keep `Free` as the evaluation lane
- Make `Pro` the main paid offer at `$12/mo`
- Treat `Team` as manual pilot only until collaboration is stronger
- Sell coherence, structure, and export handoff as the reason to pay
